# Customer Insights & Recommendation Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior and sales performance using Power BI. The dashboard provides valuable insights into customer demographics, sales trends, payment methods, shopping malls, and product categories to support business decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior
- Track monthly sales
- Understand payment preferences
- Segment customers 
- Compare shopping mall performance
- Provide business recommendations

---

## Dashboard Features

### KPIs
- Total Customers
- Total Sales
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

### Visualizations
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Quantity by Category
- Payment Method Distribution
- Customer Distribution
- Shopping Mall Performance
- Top Products
- Customer Segment
### Filters
- Top Products
- Shopping Mall
- Category

---

## Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel/CSV

---

## Business Insights

- Highest revenue months identified
- Best-performing shopping malls
- Most preferred payment methods
- Customer age segmentation
- Sales category analysis
